## 4. Ancient Versions

<b>Septuagint (LXX)</b>[^12] 
 
* ἀλάβαστρος, ‘vase’: 2 Kgs 21:13;
* εὐοδόω (pass.), ‘to prosper, be successful’: 2 Chron 35:13; <!--also LXX-Ant-->
* κόλπος, ‘bosom’: Prov 19:24; 26:15;
* πυξίον, ‘tablet’, as writing material: 2 Kgs 21:13 LXX<small><sup>Ant</sup></small>.

<b>A.1</b> In the LXX of 2 Chronicles, εὐοδόω (pass.) represents the Hebrew verb
<span dir="rtl">צלח</span> <i>hiph</i>, ‘to be successful’, in 7:11; 13:12; 18:11; 20:20; 24:20; 31:21; 32:30. In 2 Chron 35:13, <span dir="rtl">ובצלחות</span> was apparently also interpreted as a form of the verb <span dir="rtl">צלח</span>: ‘and it (the cooking) was a success’ (NETS).

[^12]: The translations of the Greek are based on <i>GELS</i>. For LXX<sup><small>Ant</small></sup>, see Fernández Marcos & Busto Saiz 1992.
